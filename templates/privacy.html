Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not ask for bank account details or card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to show you summaries of your spending. We may also use your email address to contact you about your account.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and passwords are never kept in plain text. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only if required by law, or with service providers who help us run Spendly and are bound to keep it confidential.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to make the service work. We do not use cookies for advertising.

6. Your Rights

You can view, edit or delete your expense data at any time. You may also ask us to delete your account and the data associated with it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly means you accept the updated policy.
